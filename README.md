# Kotoba: Japanese Travel Food

A small offline web app for learning Japanese food vocabulary on iPhone. Tap the picture to hear the word.

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks or external services
- Works offline after the first launch
- Words are listed in `words.json`, with pictures in `images/` and recordings in `audio/`

To install: open the site in Safari on iPhone, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
